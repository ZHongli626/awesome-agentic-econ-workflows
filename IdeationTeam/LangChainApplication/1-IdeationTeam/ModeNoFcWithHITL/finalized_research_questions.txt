QUESTION:
How do varying levels of AI exposure impact occupational mobility, unemployment duration, and the balance between automation and meaningful human work during economic downturns?

THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK:
Labor economics and theories of technological change, integrating Labor Market Theory, Technological Determinism, and Human Capital Theory.

RATIONALE:
This question addresses critical gaps in understanding the labor market dynamics influenced by AI, particularly during economic crises, providing empirical evidence that can inform policy and labor practices.

METHODOLOGY:
Quantitative analysis of labor market data, Surveys of workforce experiences, Case studies on AI integration in industries

EXPECTED IMPACT:
The research will contribute to labor economics by elucidating the effects of AI on employment, guiding policymakers in creating adaptive labor strategies.

FEASIBILITY:
Feasible with access to labor market data and collaboration with industry stakeholders.

QUESTION:

RATIONALE:
This question addresses the growing prevalence of AI companions and their potential psychological impacts, contributing to the understanding of human-AI dynamics.

METHODOLOGY:
Longitudinal studies tracking mental health outcomes, Surveys on social behavior changes, Qualitative interviews exploring user experiences

EXPECTED IMPACT:
Insights will inform AI design and ethical considerations, enhancing user well-being and guiding future AI development.

FEASIBILITY:
Feasible with access to diverse user groups and psychological assessment tools.
