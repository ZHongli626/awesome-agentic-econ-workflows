RANK 1 (Priority Score: 0.85)
======================================================================

QUESTION:
How does AI exposure impact unemployment duration and job transitions across various sectors during economic downturns, and what ethical frameworks can guide organizations in implementing AI systems to support affected workers?

THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK:
Labor Market Theory, Ethical Decision-Making Theory, Technological Disruption Theory

RATIONALE:
This question synthesizes the impact of AI on unemployment with the ethical considerations of its implementation, addressing both economic and moral dimensions. It fills critical literature gaps regarding sector-specific analysis and ethical frameworks for AI in workforce transitions.

METHODOLOGY:
Quantitative analysis of unemployment data across sectors, Qualitative interviews with policymakers and organizations, Case studies on successful AI implementations in job transitions, Surveys assessing worker experiences, Comparative analysis of ethical frameworks

EXPECTED IMPACT:
Insights for policymakers to tailor support systems for workers affected by AI, and actionable ethical guidelines for organizations deploying AI.

FEASIBILITY:
Moderate feasibility due to the availability of data and the potential for collaboration with organizations and policymakers.

EXPECTED IMPACT:
