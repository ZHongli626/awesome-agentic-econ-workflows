RANK 1 (Priority Score: 0.8)
======================================================================

QUESTION:
How do socioeconomic disparities and generational perceptions of AI influence job displacement resilience and adaptability strategies across demographic groups?

THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK:
Social Stratification Theory, Generational Theory

RATIONALE:
This question integrates socioeconomic factors and generational perceptions to provide a holistic understanding of how different demographic groups respond to AI-induced job displacement. It addresses gaps in understanding resilience and adaptability strategies, which are crucial for effective workforce development.

METHODOLOGY:
Surveys to assess perceptions across age groups, Interviews with displaced workers, Statistical analysis of socioeconomic data, Focus groups for qualitative insights, Case studies of successful adaptability interventions

EXPECTED IMPACT:
The research will inform targeted interventions that consider both socioeconomic status and generational perspectives, enhancing resilience and adaptability in the workforce.

FEASIBILITY:
Feasible with access to diverse demographic data and collaboration with workforce development organizations.

RANK 3 (Priority Score: 0.7)
======================================================================

QUESTION:
What is the effectiveness of upskilling programs in reducing unemployment rates among workers displaced by AI, and how can these programs be tailored to meet the needs of diverse demographic groups?

THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK:
Workforce Development Theory, Skills Gap Theory

RATIONALE:
This question focuses on evaluating and improving upskilling programs, which are essential for workforce reintegration post-displacement. It emphasizes the need for tailored approaches that consider demographic diversity.

METHODOLOGY:
Program evaluation studies, Surveys of program participants, Interviews with program administrators, Comparative analysis of different upskilling models, Focus groups to gather participant feedback

EXPECTED IMPACT:
The research will identify best practices for upskilling programs, leading to more effective workforce development strategies that cater to diverse needs.

FEASIBILITY:
High feasibility, as many upskilling programs are already in place and can be evaluated.
